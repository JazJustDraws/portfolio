About


Jaz Sisouvong (she/they) is a Vietnamese Chinese and Thai Laotian American artist who hails from Woodbridge, Virginia. A senior at Virginia Commonwealth University, Jaz explores the intersections of racial representation in pop culture through illustration and sociology, where she finds communicative design and Asian/American representation as her life's calling. In her free time, she can be found crafting up her fantasy romance IP, editing a video and tampering with DAWs, learning too many languages she has no time for, or her nose buried in a book.
